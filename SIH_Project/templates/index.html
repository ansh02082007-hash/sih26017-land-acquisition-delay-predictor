Layer-by-Layer Architectural Specifications

Every neural-network specific choice earns its complexity live during inference and retraining:

1. Entity Embeddings

• Dimension: min(12, (N+1)//2)
• Inputs: PIN Codes & Districts
• Dropout: 0.1 post-concat
• Why: Avoids one-hot sparsity; projects coordinates into geographic clusters.

2. Wide Component

• Mapping: Linear(21, 16)
• Inputs: One-Hot + Numericals
• Activation: Linear memorization
• Why: Captures direct correlations without non-linear distortion.

3. Deep Layer 1

• Architecture: Linear(45, 64)
• Activation: GELU + BatchNorm1d
• Dropout: 0.3 (Tapering)
• Why: Learns foundational interactions across spatial & demographic features.

4. Deep Layer 2 & 3

• Architecture: 64 → 32 → 16
• Activation: GELU + BatchNorm1d
• Dropout: 0.2 → 0.1 (Tapering)
• Why: Funnels complex cross-features into a high-level representation bottleneck.

5. Fused Representation

• Dimension: 32 (16 Wide + 16 Deep)
• Type: Latent Shared Vector
• Phase 2 Hook: TabNet Sparse Attention
• Why: Forces multi-task co-regularization between likelihood & severity.

6. Head A & Head B

• Head A: Linear → Sigmoid → Prob
• Head B: Linear → Softplus → Mos ≥ 0
• Loss: Focal (γ=2) + Masked Huber
• Why: Guarantees non-negative months; Focal loss handles severe class skew.
